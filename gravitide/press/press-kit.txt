Turn space debris into a weapon in a one-finger physics arcade game for iPhone.

Gravitide is a one-finger physics arcade game for iPhone. Hold the screen to draw your ship and nearby debris into orbit, charge a fragment, then release it as a projectile. The same gravity that helps you attack also moves your ship, so every throw is a balance of aim, timing and survival. The campaign spans 15 expeditions, five sectors and 55 waves, with upgrades, unlockable modules and boss encounters. Version 1.2 adds a hands-on flight lesson, clearer aiming feedback, slower action when a throw is ready, and Spanish and German alongside English and Russian. The first three expeditions are free; one purchase unlocks the remaining twelve.
