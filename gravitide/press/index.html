THE GAME

Aim with gravity.
Live with the pull.

Gravitide is a one-finger physics arcade game for iPhone. Hold the screen to draw your ship and nearby debris into orbit, charge a fragment, then release it as a projectile. The same gravity that helps you attack also moves your ship, so every throw is a balance of aim, timing and survival.

The campaign spans 15 expeditions, five sectors and 55 waves, with upgrades, unlockable modules and boss encounters. The first three expeditions are free; one purchase unlocks the remaining twelve.

What changed in 1.2

A hands-on lesson for moving, throwing and dodging. Clearer aiming feedback, slower action when a throw is ready, and a calmer opening expedition. Spanish and German join English and Russian.
