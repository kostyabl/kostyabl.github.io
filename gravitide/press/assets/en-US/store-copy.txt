Gravitide: Gravity Throw

One-Finger Physics Arcade

Turn space debris into a weapon. Learn to steer, aim and release with one finger. Play the first 3 expeditions free; unlock the other 12 with one purchase.

One finger. A field of debris. A shot only you can create.

In Gravitide, gravity is your weapon. Touch and hold to pull your ship and nearby debris into orbit. Charge a fragment, read its flight path and release at just the right moment. A drifting rock becomes the shot that clears your way. Keep your ship safe as you steer it with the same pull.

RECONNECT THE LOST SECTORS
Fly through 15 expeditions and 55 waves across 5 sectors: the Debris Belt, the Shattered Station, the Beacon Reach, the Tidal Rift and the Pulsar Network. Navigate barriers, ride currents, time your throws between pulsar flares and defeat the Wardens.

MAKE EVERY THROW YOUR OWN
Choose upgrades between waves and unlock three starting modules. Ricochet extends a throw after a wall bounce. Long Echo changes the field you leave behind. Singularity combines debris into a heavier projectile. Pick one module and discover how it changes your approach.

READ THE ARENA
Anchors hold their ground. Heavies drift into your path. Devourers hunt your ammunition. Success comes from choosing your target, finding an opening and releasing your charge with care.
